MLM Equation Builder

Build your multilevel model. Generate equations, explanations, and reporting text.

Build your model

1. Level 1 Predictors

Specify predictors measured at Level 1. Each predictor can have its own centering and random-slope setting.

2. Level 2 Predictors

Specify group-level predictors. Each Level 2 predictor can be entered uncentered or grand-mean centered.

3. Random Effects

Specify variation in intercepts and covariance among the selected random effects.

4. Cross-Level Interactions

After defining Level 1 and Level 2 predictors, choose which Level 2 predictor predicts which Level 1 slope.

v0.9: model setup is organized in sequence—Level 1 predictors, Level 2 predictors, random effects, then cross-level interactions.
